About

From the feed room to the data

I studied Animal Husbandry at Bangladesh Agricultural University and am now finishing an MSc in Animal Nutrition there. My training is hands-on: broiler feeding trials, rearing black soldier fly, superworm and mealworm larvae, proximate and NIR analysis, and ration formulation.

I have always been fascinated by how data can help us forecast and predict: how heavy an animal is, how a pen will finish, what a new feed will do. That curiosity pulled me, one step at a time, into machine learning and then deep learning models, which I taught myself alongside my lab work.

Next, I am looking for a PhD or another suitable higher-study opportunity where animal science and computational methods meet, in precision livestock farming or computer vision for livestock.
