Ongoing MSc thesis

Cattle live weight from photographs

Image-based live weight estimation of cattle in Bangladesh: a comparison of deep learning and machine learning models.

StatusOngoing, expected November 2026
DataPublic Cow Images Dataset: 513 cattle, 4 photos each
ToolsPyTorch, timm, YOLO11, scikit-learn
DepartmentAnimal Nutrition, BAU

In short

  • The question. Most cattle in Bangladesh are weighed by eye, because scales are costly and hard to move. Can ordinary side-view photos take the scale's place?
  • The approach. Pretrained deep learning networks (CNNs and vision transformers) read the photos directly; classical models use body-outline measurements cut out with YOLO11. All are compared with a no-photo baseline.
  • The test. Every model is scored only on animals it has never seen, with all four photos of an animal kept together.
Results will be shared here after the thesis defence.
