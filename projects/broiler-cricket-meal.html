Broilers fed cricket meal

Broiler growth and feed efficiency with dietary cricket meal: an open trial dataset, checked and reanalysed.

Code and notebooks on GitHub
Data35-day trial, 6 diets, 24 pens (Mendeley Data)
Unit of analysisThe pen
Toolspandas, SciPy, scikit-learn

In short

  • Checked first. Recomputed every value in the workbook and found gains that contradict the weights in 14 of 120 pen-weeks.
  • Analysed fairly. Diets compared with the pen, not the bird, as the experimental unit.
  • Forecast ahead. A simple model on day-28 weight forecast the final weight of unseen pens within 1.3%.

Data: doi:10.17632/rj5fybnzh6.1. Analysis and figures are my own.
