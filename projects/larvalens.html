LarvaLens

Counting, measuring, weighing and sexing black soldier fly larvae from photos.

Code on GitHubSoftware DOIKaggle notebooks
DataOpen larva dataset (Zenodo, CC BY 4.0)
ModelsYOLOv8 segmentation, ResNet-18
StatusReleased, citable

In short

  • What. A complete open pipeline: find each larva, outline it, measure its length and width, and estimate its weight and sex.
  • What is different. Models are tested on whole rearing batches they never saw, the situation a real insect farm faces.
  • What I found. Weight can be learned straight from the photo (R² 0.70 on unseen batches), but sex cannot be told reliably from a single photo.

Cite: Patwary, M. R. (2026). LarvaLens (Version 1.0.1) [Computer software]. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23325495. Data: Geissmann (2024), CC BY 4.0; study of Nawoya et al. (2025).
